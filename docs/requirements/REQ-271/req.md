# Type a kidney calculus tag with size and calyx

Order: 6 of 9
Depends on: 3 (normal-organ tags)

## Purpose
As a radiologist, I can type L or R with a size (5-14.99) and a calyx (u, um, m, lm, l) and get findings and impression.

## Why
Kidney calculus wording and impression depend on side, size and calyx. Source: Abbreviation Rules v4 ([L,R]5-14.99 section).

## Behaviour and acceptance
Acceptance:
- Side L or R gives left or right kidney wording
- Size is inserted as "... mm calculus"
- Calyx u, um, m, lm, l gives upper, upper middle, middle, lower middle, lower calyx
- Impression gives the side and single calculus or bilateral calculi
- Size outside 5-14.99 is reported as not covered
