# Type liver size and get enlargement grade

Order: 5 of 9
Depends on: 3 (normal-organ tags)

## Purpose
As a radiologist, I can type Li with a size (e.g. Li15.30) and get the enlargement grade, size and impression.

## Why
Liver size gives grade wording and impression. Source: Abbreviation Rules v4 (Li section), sample report.

## Behaviour and acceptance
Acceptance:
- Li15.30 gives "Liver is mildly enlarged in size (15.30 cm)..." and the impression "Mild hepatomegaly" (as in sample report)
- Grades: mildly, mild to moderately, moderately, moderately to markedly, markedly
- The grade follows the size range for the patient's sex
- A size within the normal range gives normal liver wording (to confirm)
- Impression uses the matching grade of hepatomegaly
